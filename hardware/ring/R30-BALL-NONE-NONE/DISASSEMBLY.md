# R30-BALL-NONE-NONE Disassembly Baseline

This teardown path is part of the publication baseline for the ball+Hall ring.

## Safety First

- Disconnect USB power before opening the ring.
- Remove the battery before prolonged heat or solvent exposure.
- Do not allow loose magnets or steel balls near the pouch cell during service.

## Intended Removal Order

1. Open the shell at the primary service seam.
2. Remove the battery before extracting the ball and roller hardware.
3. Remove the ball as a discrete wear/cleaning component.
4. Remove rollers and magnets without cutting the shell or deforming the socket.
5. Remove PCB or Hall-sensor assembly after the moving parts are documented.
6. Remove the dome click without destroying its seat.

## Replacement Expectations

- Ball and rollers are wear items and should be replaceable.
- A single failed Hall channel should not require shell destruction.
- Battery replacement must remain independent from the ball socket.

## Failure Conditions

If the only practical way to clean debris from the ball path is to crack the
shell, or if battery replacement requires disturbing permanently bonded roller
assemblies, the design fails this baseline.
