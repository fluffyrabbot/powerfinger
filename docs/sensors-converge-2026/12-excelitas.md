# Excelitas — Tier B

**What they make:** Photonics — VCSEL emitters, photodiodes, IR detectors,
LEDs, custom optical modules, scientific imaging components. Industrial /
medical / defense markets primarily.

## Why they matter for PowerFinger

Same shape as ams OSRAM, narrower scope. Excelitas only matters if the
deferred **guidance-laser** capability moves into an active validation lane —
the "red dot on the surface" feature in `../README.md` §"Deferred
Capabilities."

Their VCSEL portfolio could supply the laser emitter; their photodiode
portfolio could supply the OCR camera's IR-illumination feedback if that
capability is also pursued.

Neither is in the active plan today. This is forward-looking only.

## The ask

1. **Minimum viable:** Datasheet pointer for their lowest-power Class-1
   eye-safe VCSEL emitter at sub-mA average current. That's the gating
   regulatory question.
2. **Stretch:** None today. Re-engage when the feature is active.

## What you bring

- Almost nothing immediate. Excelitas does not engage at small scales.

## Conversation cues

- If the booth foregrounds **defense / scientific** — wrong day; ask for a
  consumer-photonics contact.
- If the booth shows **VCSEL** demos — that's the right team.

## Risks / why they might say no

- Volume thresholds. They may not be willing to engage at all.
- Eye-safety class limits could disqualify a wearable guidance laser
  outright; this is a feature-killing question worth asking even if
  the booth conversation goes nowhere else.

## Minimum viable outcome from a 5-minute booth visit

- One Class-1 VCSEL part number and its average-current spec.

## Post-show follow-up path

1. None planned. Re-engage only if guidance-laser feature activates.
