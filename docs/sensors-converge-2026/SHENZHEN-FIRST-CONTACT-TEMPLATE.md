# Shenzhen / Seeed First Contact Template

Use this as the repo-local outbound template for the first Shenzhen prototype
house or Seeed Fusion / Propagate contact. Keep the placeholders honest. Do not
add readiness, yield, certification, OCR, companion-app, or secondary-variant
claims unless a current packet source file backs them.

## Subject

PowerFinger USB-HUB PCB assembly quote + enclosure/connector DFM review

## Message

Hello [factory / Seeed contact],

I am preparing a first factory review packet for PowerFinger, an open-source
assistive input hardware project. I would like your feedback on the attached or
linked source packet under two separate scopes:

1. `USB-HUB` send-now quote path
   - Please quote PCB fabrication and assembly for the current `USB-HUB` source
     packet.
   - Please review the SOFNG USB-05 direct-plug USB-A connector topology,
     enclosure source, connector retention approach, and serviceability path for
     DFM feedback.
   - Current repo-backed electrical status: schematic ERC=0, PCB DRC=0,
     unconnected=0, and schematic/PCB parity=0.
   - Open evidence items: printed host fit, adjacent-port clearance,
     clamp-hole alignment, service-hatch reach, and connector-retention
     observations are not measured yet.

2. `R30-OLED-NONE-NONE` optional annex
   - Please treat the ring files as DFM/pre-fab review only.
   - Do not quote ring PCB fabrication or assembly from the annex.
   - Current repo-backed electrical status is recorded in
     `hardware/ring/R30-OLED-NONE-NONE/kicad/CURRENT-VIOLATIONS.md`; generated
     output review is recorded in
     `hardware/ring/R30-OLED-NONE-NONE/kicad/FABRICATION-OUTPUTS.md`. The KiCad
     gate and active-BOM/POS review are clean, but the ring remains annex-only
     until board-house output constraints and physical fit/stackup evidence
     close.
   - Useful feedback would be BOM costability, PAW3204 optical sensor sourcing
     and assembly concerns, off-board USB service-pad fixture access, off-board
     replaceable battery harness or fixture serviceability, shell
     serviceability, focal-distance risks, and issues that must close before a
     ring PCB quote is appropriate.

Please keep any proposed substitutions explicit: manufacturer part number,
package or footprint impact, source, MOQ, and whether the change affects
serviceability or the editable hardware source that would be returned for
CERN-OHL-S publication.

The packet intentionally excludes secondary ring variants, wand, puck, OCR, and
companion-app product features.

Thank you,

[name]
[project/repo link or source packet link]
[preferred contact channel]
