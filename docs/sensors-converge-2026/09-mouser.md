# Mouser Electronics — Tier B

**What they make:** Major component distributor. No products of their own; they
move other vendors' inventory. Headline sponsor of Sensors Converge 2026.
Active editorial / merchandising operation (Mouser Empowering Innovation
articles, Methods Magazine, sponsored reference-design content).

## Why they matter for PowerFinger

Mouser's role is **distribution and merchandising**, not silicon. The value
of the conversation is twofold:

1. **"Buy the BOM" merchandising.** Mouser has historically built sponsored
   landing pages for high-visibility open-hardware projects (Adafruit
   collaborations, Hackaday Prize finalists, etc.). A "buy the PowerFinger
   Standard ring BOM in one click" page collapses the activation energy for
   builders dramatically — exactly the lever
   [`../REFERENCE-MANUFACTURERS.md`](../REFERENCE-MANUFACTURERS.md) describes
   wanting at Tier 1 (DIY) and Tier 2 (small batch).
2. **Editorial visibility.** Their *Empowering Innovation Together* program
   features projects in articles that reach hundreds of thousands of engineers.
   Accessibility-themed content is editorially attractive.

## The ask

1. **Minimum viable:** Confirmation that they offer a "BOM list / project
   bundle" merchandising feature, and a contact for it.
2. **Stretch:** Editorial pitch — accessibility-flavored open-hardware project
   feature in their newsletter or blog. Possibly tied to a Hackaday.io project
   page launch (`../OUTREACH.md` Tier 2 plan).

## What you bring

- A clean BOM that's already documented down to manufacturer part numbers (the
  hardware tree's BOM-backed publication packets cover this).
- An accessibility narrative that fits their editorial preferences.
- A community-driven distribution surface (GitHub, Hackaday) that gives Mouser's
  sponsored content a credible audience.

## Conversation cues

- If the booth has **"Empowering Innovation Together"** signage, that's the
  editorial program — go straight to a marketing/editorial contact.
- If the booth foregrounds **distribution sales / line cards** — wrong team
  for this conversation; ask for marketing.

## Risks / why they might say no

- Mouser's editorial slots are limited and competitive.
- The CERN-OHL-S license is unusual; their merchandising team may need to be
  educated on what it does and does not require of buyers (it requires
  publication only on conveyance of modified designs, not on simple component
  purchase).

## Minimum viable outcome from a 5-minute booth visit

- Editorial / marketing contact email.
- Pointer to their open-hardware merchandising feature (URL, format, examples).

## Post-show follow-up path

1. Email contact with one-pager + a Hackaday.io project URL once it exists.
2. Cross-reference with [`../OUTREACH.md`](../OUTREACH.md) §Tier 2 (Hackaday)
   — these two pipelines reinforce each other.
3. The Mouser BOM landing page becomes the canonical "how to buy" link from
   the project README at small batch volumes.
