# Choosing a prototype partner for PowerFinger

The first purchase worth considering is a working comparison fixture, a recorded bench run, and the right to reproduce both. PowerFinger has editable hardware and firmware sources. Physical ring fit, click usability, optical tracking on a tray, power consumption and achieved unit cost still need evidence. The $10 base-ring and $25 per-variant limits apply to prototype parts. Quote engineering, fixture fabrication, assembly, sourcing, freight and participant work separately.

## Choose the route by the missing capability

| Situation | Recommended partner and scope | Evidence that earns the next expense |
|---|---|---|
| You have no bench access and travel is tentative | Hire a California prototype engineer or rehabilitation-engineering collaborator to own one small work order. Ask for an interchangeable ring and sled around a working optical head, an external switch, a remote witnessed run and return of the fixture. A donor head can answer the ergonomic question. | The engineer names the person running the bench, demonstrates raw signed motion and button events, ships the fixtures and returns the files and failed runs. |
| The main uncertainty is who can wear the device or operate the switch | Pair the local builder with an assistive-technology practitioner and willing users. Ask about borrowing an appropriate switch. Budget participant compensation and accessibility accommodations explicitly. | Each user can try the three conditions with their normal support, and their preference and assistance needs are recorded. |
| You have a clear test, an exact optical part or an agreed sample hunt, and can attend physical iterations | Work directly with a Shenzhen prototyping lab and an engineer who can modify a fixture at the bench. Use a Guangdong supplier visit when the specific supplier owns a lens, assembly, or enclosure process that needs examination. | Before booking around the project, obtain a named host, confirmed bench access, a parts list, a written sample or bring-your-own-board plan, and agreement to return editable modifications and raw results. |
| Travel falls through, or ongoing rework, sample consolidation or inspection needs local ownership abroad | A California-based representative with an identified engineer in Guangdong can be worth the fee. Give them the same work order and require a remote witnessed test. | Their quote identifies the actual build site, pass-through costs, markup, responsibility for failed samples and who answers technical questions after delivery. |
| Parts and drawings are locked, and the only decision is price for a defined assembly | Request comparable direct quotes from a prototype house. A broker adds value only if they can identify a concrete sourcing, language, inspection, or logistics problem they will own. | Quotes at the same quantities and revision separate component costs, engineering, test and shipping. Critical substitutes include traceable MPNs, dimensional drawings and samples. |

Ability Tools lists device-lending resources for people trying assistive technology. Ask about eligibility and a possible practitioner introduction. A lending center's listing does not establish access to participants or a product-testing service. Ask separately for DFM review, fixture work and assembly. Confirm staffing, lab availability, exact optics and small-quantity terms directly before arranging travel. These are capability leads, with no engagement or quotation assumed. ## What to pitch

> PowerFinger is an offline, repairable pointing device for people with limited wrist movement. We want to learn whether a person can rest their hand, move a pointer, select text and drag on a tray. We have a ring design and three ways to test the same optics. We are looking for an engineering partner who can put those fixtures on a bench with us, show us what fails, and return the editable fixes. The base ring must cost under $10 in prototype parts.

Bring or send the revision identifier, a one-page drawing or unpowered shell, the exact sensor/lens/emitter requirements, one local test document, and the [comparison worksheet](BENCH-INPUT-COMPARISON.md). A video from a donor fixture should be labelled as donor evidence. When physical evidence is unavailable, show the design and ask to reproduce a defined measurement. Keep the hub's existing quote path and the ring's DFM-only annex distinct. The three-condition sled is a test fixture. [Factory packet](sensors-converge-2026/SHENZHEN-SEEED-QUOTE-PACKET.md)

## A first work order either partner can quote

1. List what the lab already has and what it will purchase. Require exact optical kit contents, assembly drawings, contact force, source, MOQ and an estimate at two and ten units. Identify the responsible engineer and agree on separate charges for engineering, prototypes and rework. Define who retains and ships good and failed samples.
2. Start with an unpowered fit mock and a powered, off-body optical fixture. Include the board, cell dummy, harness bend, lens, feet and reopenable lid in the fit check. Return dimensions, collision photos, and the observed optical gap under load. On the powered fixture, show rail readings, acquisition of sensor identity, emitter operation, reversible X/Y, button release and raw HID logs.
3. Use the same optical head and controller for the ring/dome, ring/separate-switch and sled/separate-switch trials. Run the operator check before scheduling participants. Arrange user trials separately with permission. Remote manufacturing footage can establish device behavior. Comfort and independent use require user observations.
4. Return a tagged source revision or patch, native KiCad and OpenSCAD files, firmware configuration, fixture wiring, sourceable BOM with alternatives clearly marked, raw logs and photographs with a scale. Hardware changes must be publishable under CERN-OHL-S-2.0, firmware under MIT, and the delivered device must function offline. Agree in writing on editable source and fixture ownership before paying for design work.
5. Accept or return the work against the worksheet. A failed test with reproducible evidence is a useful outcome. Release the R30 for a board order only after engineering review, manufacturing inputs and the repo's physical gates justify it. Defer tooling and volume commitments until repeat builds meet the same tests.

**Recommendation.** Use remote quotations and a bounded California engineering or AT collaboration to establish the human comparison if affordable. A Guangdong trip has the most value once a named lab can put parts, fixtures and an engineer in the same room. If there is no credible hardware partner or special access on offer, keep travel optional and spend the prototype budget on the comparison itself.
